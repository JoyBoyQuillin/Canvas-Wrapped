# Privacy Policy — Canvas Wrapped

**Last updated:** [fill in date before publishing]

Canvas Wrapped is a browser extension that generates a personalized,
Wrapped-style summary of your own Canvas LMS activity — grades, courses,
and assignment history — directly in your browser.

## What data the extension reads

While you're signed into Canvas, the extension reads the following from
your own Canvas account, using your existing logged-in session:

- Your name
- Your enrolled courses
- Your grades and computed scores
- Your assignment and submission data (due dates, submitted dates, scores)

The extension only ever accesses **your own** Canvas data. It does not
access other students' data, instructor tools, or any part of Canvas
beyond what your own account can already see.

## What data is stored, and where

The data above is cached in your browser's local extension storage
(`browser.storage.local`), keyed to your Canvas site and user ID, so the
Wrapped summary can be regenerated without re-fetching from Canvas every
time you open it.

- This storage lives only on your device.
- It is never transmitted to any server we operate — we don't operate
  any server. The extension has no backend.
- It is automatically deleted if you uninstall the extension.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.
- We don't use your data for advertising or ad targeting.
- We don't use your data to build profiles beyond generating your own
  Wrapped summary.
- We don't collect your Canvas password, login credentials, or any
  authentication information — the extension relies on your browser's
  existing Canvas session and never sees or touches your credentials.
- We don't collect health, financial, or payment information.
- We don't track your browsing activity outside of Canvas pages.

## Optional: copying data to an AI tool

The extension includes an optional feature that copies a summary of your
Wrapped data to your clipboard, formatted as a prompt, so you can
optionally paste it into an AI assistant of your choice for personalized
recommendations. This action:

- Only happens if you click the button — nothing is copied automatically.
- Is disclosed at the point of use, since pasting that data into a
  third-party AI tool shares it with that tool's provider, subject to
  *their* privacy policy and data practices, which we don't control.

## Permissions

The extension requests host permission for `fiu.instructure.com` in
order to read your Canvas data as described above. It requests no other
host permissions.
